How do I get to the North-East?

Most trips start in Guwahati (GAU), with direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Chennai and Hyderabad. The Anini trip starts in Dibrugarh (DIB), and the Mizoram trip in Aizawl (AJL).

Do I need a permit?

Indian citizens need an Inner Line Permit for Arunachal Pradesh and Mizoram. ILPs are included on both Arunachal trips, and we guide you through Mizoram's. Foreign nationals need a Protected Area Permit for Arunachal and must register on arrival in Mizoram. Rules change, so we confirm everything when you book. Meghalaya needs no permit.

Is the North-East safe to travel?

Yes. Meghalaya, Tawang, the Dibang Valley and Mizoram are calm and used to visitors. Himanshu works with stays and drivers he knows, and you can reach him directly throughout the trip.

How fit do I need to be?

Each trip has a difficulty rating out of 5. The Mizoram and Anini trips are 2: mostly drives with short walks. The Meghalaya and Tawang trips are 3: Meghalaya has steep stairs down to the root bridges (about 3,500 steps to Nongriat), and Tawang goes above 4,000 m, where the air is thin. Ask Himanshu if you're unsure which suits you.

When is the best time to go?

October to April suits most routes. Monsoon (June–September) is spectacular for Meghalaya's waterfalls but slows mountain roads. Kaziranga (the Day 8 add-on) is closed from May to October. Each trip card shows its best months.

Why do some trips say "price on request"?

Fares depend on group size, dates and vehicle. Send an enquiry and Himanshu replies with an exact quote, usually within a day.

Do you run private trips?

Yes. Couples, families and friend groups can book any route as a private departure on their own dates. Corporate offsites are welcome too.

How do I pay and confirm my booking?

Pay a 30% advance to confirm your seats; the balance is due just before the trip starts. We accept UPI and bank transfer only, and every payment comes with a GST invoice from ADVENTURES WITH HIMANSHU.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please send cancellations in writing on WhatsApp or email. Refunds depend on how close to departure you cancel:

30 days or more before departureFull advance, minus ₹2,000 per person processing fee
15 to 29 days before50% of the advance
7 to 14 days before25% of the advance
Less than 7 days, or no-showNo refund

Bookings for festive-season departures are non-refundable, because stays and transport are paid for well in advance. Refunds go back to the account you paid from.

Can I change my travel dates?

Yes, you can change your dates once, free of charge, subject to confirmation and the group size on the new departure.

Is there a minimum group size?

Group departures run with a minimum of 6 travellers (Innova Crysta) or 10 travellers (Force Traveller). If a departure doesn't reach the minimum, you can move to another date or take a full refund of what you've paid.

What if weather or landslides affect the trip?

Mountain weather and roads in the North-East can change quickly. If a route becomes unsafe or a permit area closes, we switch to the best safe alternative. Any extra cost, such as an added night, is discussed with you before it is spent.
